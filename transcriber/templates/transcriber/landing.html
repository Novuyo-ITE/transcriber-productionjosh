FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Which languages are supported?
NOVUscribe provides dedicated high-accuracy support for Luganda and Lingala, alongside native support for English, French, Swahili, and 90+ global languages.
How does automatic speaker identification work?
The system analyzes vocal acoustic patterns to automatically separate different speakers in conversations and label them (e.g., Speaker 1, Speaker 2), making multi-person meetings easy to read.
What media formats and links can I transcribe?
You can upload audio files (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC), video recordings (MP4, MKV, AVI), paste direct YouTube links, or record live through your microphone.
Do I need an account to transcribe audio?
Yes, creating a free account is required to access transcription services, real-time live mic streaming, and to store your past transcripts securely.
